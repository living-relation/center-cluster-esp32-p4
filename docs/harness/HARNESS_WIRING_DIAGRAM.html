★ = TrackCluster harness. From Waveshare schematic J8 + Kconfig. Avoid GPIO34–38 (strap) and GPIO39–44 (microSD).
Build notes. Plug the 40-pin screw-terminal adapter onto center J8 (2×20, 2.54 mm). Use ferrules or bare wire in the Phoenix clamps. Keep CAN and UART pairs twisted; star-ground all nodes at the buck (−). SN65HVD230 is passive hardware — check onboard 120 Ω before adding a bus terminator. Flash side clusters on USB-C only; do not monitor GPIO43/44 during driving. Controls: panel DT leads land on T18/T19/T21/T31/T32/T34/T36; 100 nF to GND at each terminal for debounce.
