Build notes. Keep the CAN pair twisted from the ECU branch to the transceiver; 120 Ω termination at both bus ends. Keep each UART TX/RX twisted with a ground reference. Star-ground all three displays and the transceiver back to the buck output ground. Flash side displays over USB-C — do not share a UART console on GPIO43/44 at runtime. Controls (C5–C7): wire directly from J8 header pins to the panel-mounted DT connectors — no board-local JST intermediate needed. Keep each encoder's A/B/SW + GND within its own single connector (C6 or C7). Add a 100 nF ceramic cap per signal to GND at the J8 header end to debounce mechanical contacts on all three devices. Verify before ordering: confirm center J8 is standard 2×20 / 2.54 mm / 0.64 mm square posts, and confirm each side board's UART connector pitch on your exact PCB revision — keep the net map above and substitute only the local connector family if it differs.
